# Rev10 real-photo diagnosis and translation

All six actual photographs were opened visually before modelling. All six original photographs were retrieved, including R4's 2,048×877 unresized original in addition to its embedded cropped thumbnail. Original bytes/hashes and author/source/rights are retained. The reference Doc is read-only. No reference photograph becomes a scene texture, skybox or artist artwork.

R1, BohunkaNika, CC BY-SA 4.0: repeated functional black tanks with lids and pipe connections, concrete edge thickness, recessed doors, corrugated shade, sagging services and laundry. It is a utility/fabric reference. Translate purposeful tank/pipe assemblies and connected roof-edge construction; avoid covering every roof with an arbitrary tank array.

R2, Guido van Helten's Dharavi project, rights unverified beyond private reference: a small roof-access room forms an immediate backdrop to an informal dance gathering; weathered cream render, worn blue openings, utility lines, low parapet and human-scale roof furnishings. Translate the low near roof-edge/readable domestic backdrop and staggered taller buildings behind. Do not copy people, lamps, artwork or proprietary image pixels. Our locked 180 actors/1,088 m² floor cannot reproduce the exact intimacy of this much smaller gathering.

R3, Jakub Hałun, CC BY-SA 4.0: an actual close-facing, deteriorated courtyard with continuous accessible galleries, deep door recesses, exposed beams, plaster layers, drains/wires and laundry. It is not an immaculate palace. Translate the spatial depth, shared gallery circulation and mismatched levels; omit the heritage arches/ornament, abandoned hazards and floor debris.

R4, Philos Records, rights unverified beyond private reference: an informal rooftop performance with people at arm's length from portable equipment, low edges and a compact working roof backdrop. Translate the equipment/people-to-architecture relationship in the existing screen/crowd composition. No new truss, performer stage, lighting string or replacement audience.

R5, Jakub Hałun, CC BY-SA 4.0: the supplied photograph is a close elevated view of market-facing galleries, rough shared terraces, small roofs/canopies, stairs, tanks/barrels, patched surfaces and domestic/commercial use. It does not show an entire skyline. Translate the interlocking levels, exposed roof thicknesses and purposeful small-scale use into the side/background roofscape; do not add a market across the protected screen/floor.

R6, NexusMoves, CC BY-SA 3.0: a Bronx ground-level dance circle, not a roof. The spectators create a loose, porous human edge. Use only a context comparison; all original C180 IDs, bodies and footprints stay exact. No crowd redistribution is authorised.

## Diagnosis of actual Rev09

The original 24-page PDF and original aerial/front/facade PNGs were read. Targeted hypotheses: repeated horizontal bands, similar evenly spaced window proportions, full-height near walls and clean continuous outer enclosure make the result read as an apartment compound. Roof domestic use is sparse; the foreground is a large protected plane. Even its varied heights share one repetitive facade grammar. These are visual hypotheses, not claims that the user separately listed each criticism. Frontage coverage is not used as an art-success metric.

## Two reversible architectural studies

A — Connected working roofs: lower inhabited edge rooms and thick low parapets, deep side terraces, offset taller housing behind, deliberately open skyline gaps, short service stairs between modelled roof levels, linked drainage, sparse laundry/utility use. R2/R4 guide foreground/backdrop relationship; R1/R5 guide layered fabric. More roof identity, less uninterrupted vertical enclosure.

B — Layered residential edge: closer unequal house clusters with continuous inset galleries, deeper doorways, split upper setbacks and roof-access terraces. R3 guides gallery depth without haveli ornament; R5 guides adjoining levels. Stronger enclosure, potentially a greater courtyard reading. Both keep mixed multi-storey buildings and the identical main open floor.

Neither study changes the existing floor/crowd/screen/board/art, route or lighting. No domestic emission or fill is selected. The two results are proposals for user choice, not approvals or exact photo replicas. A photo-to-render matrix and a two-page labelled reference/current/proposal board accompany final genuine Blender renders.
