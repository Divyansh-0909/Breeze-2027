# Rev15U / Green highway navigation signs

14 November 2026; authoring began 13 November. The user requested the Indian highway-sign references' green backgrounds, white inset borders, white lettering and a matching road-sign font, while preserving the current navigation orientation and leaving the digital sponsor board alone.

The six existing physical plaques and Gullyverse header now use green #087655 and white #F5F6F0. Overpass Medium is used for the place names and SemiBold for Gullyverse. Overpass is an open-source interpretation of Highway Gothic, selected for the reference's road-sign appearance; this does not claim identification of the exact font in the photos. Font binaries, attribution, SIL Open Font License and sourced file hashes are included. Existing panel positions, rotations, scales, sizes and label anchors are preserved. The Events and Team directions are restyled as white arrow icons; Aftermovie still points up. The existing destination slots remain unchanged. Schedule remains an undecided placeholder, not a newly accepted destination.

The digital sponsor face, housing, hangers, screws, cable, spill light, shader, native animation and all 62 logo marks remain unchanged. The native scene retains 72-cell logo gaps and the 10,676-cell loop. The private browser viewer reuses the prior LED shader, atlas and sponsor manifest byte for byte. Its loop-start and loop-end pixels match, the scroll advances correctly, and mobile viewing has no overflow or console/network errors.

The active master is `/dev/shm/Gullyverse-Rev15U-Green-Highway-Navigation.blend` (216,815,099 bytes; SHA256 `1e80737ec636d5a105d6d90f1b1f654ec53bf4e196c2501bc295e1de4d947145`). It preserves 52 historical scenes and adds the 53rd. All 102 images remain packed, along with both highway-sign fonts. The four road meshes and their UV/colour attributes, rebuilt T3 potholes, original movement source, crowd, shops, murals, world, light settings and exposure remain unchanged.

Fresh-open native preservation and browser checks passed. The native recovery helper and its independent fresh-open verification are recorded in evidence/restore-check.json and evidence/restored-fresh-open.json. Native recovery uses the complete verified T2 master plus the packed T3 ground delta and the current U styling delta; see RESTORE.md and delivery/Dependency-Index.json. Original and reconstructed Blender files can have different serialization checksums while the verified scene content matches.
