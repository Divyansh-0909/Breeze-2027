# Rev15U2 / English navigation labels centred vertically

14 November 2026. At the user's request, the seven Hindi navigation labels have been removed from the active scene and all seven English labels, including Gullyverse, have been centred vertically within their existing panels. The taller 0.68 m panels, green faces, white borders, panel orientations, all arrow directions, Accommodation destination and the digital sponsor board remain as before. The sponsor screen geometry, materials, texture atlas, shader, 62 logos, 72-cell spacing and 10,676-cell loop passed native and browser preservation checks. The corrected road, movement shell, original movement/input/rendering source, crowd, shops, murals and saved lighting remain unchanged. The bilingual milestone remains intact in the preserved historical scene and its earlier Drive backup. No Hindi navigation meshes are linked into the active U2 scene.
