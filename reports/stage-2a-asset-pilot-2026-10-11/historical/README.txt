# Breeze 2027 / Gullyverse visual references

## Priority
1. Latest user-approved detailed requirements and reference images in groups 01–09 control FINAL visual direction.
2. baselines/current-implementation are BEFORE shots of the temporary 3D world, not visual targets.
3. baselines/original-website document existing interaction, content and iconic assets that must be preserved where not expressly superseded.
4. Existing project source code and docs control implementation facts; do not infer working destination routes from images.

## Groups and use
- 01-entrance: carved/rocky portal, exterior mural surfaces, GULLYVERSE spray-painted at top. Do NOT include skull/horror decoration or reproduce subway signage.
- 02-rocky-graffiti-tunnel: rough rock, graffiti for previous artists (Neha Kakkar, Chaar Diwaari, Ritviz, Khullar G, Twin Strings), progressive brighter quarry exit.
- 03-open-roof-quarry: immense excavated open-roof space, warm real sun, darker shadowed edges, physical scale.
- 04-exit-wayfinding: industrial scaffold navigation with diagonal physical boards. Quarry stage must be distant and slightly LEFT in the tunnel exit composition.
- 05-left-right-stalls: warm practical/string lights, stalls with real layouts, sparse groups; keep center walking path clear.
- 06-team-board: physical soft/pinboard with polaroids, names under photos, visible pins, team headings, central OCs and zoom-to-cluster.
- 07-contact-booth: Breeze 2027 branded festival kiosk/counter and form attached to its rear backdrop, activated at near range; preserve working form fields and behavior.
- 08-aerial-map: GTA-like camera rise from player's current pose and full-screen overview of the SAME navigable 3D quarry, world-aligned markers. No centered modal.
- 09-aftermovie: stage at far end, slightly left in tunnel-exit view; preserve existing stage model/crowd/video. ANY outer boundary crossing fades text-only location options over scene; Esc/Stay stays, selected destination fades through black.

## Important
These are art-direction references, not 3D geometry, original texture sheets or licensed production art. Do not paste photographed art/logos/people from the references into the deployed website. Create original assets and keep the project's actual Breeze artwork/content unless specifically replaced. Screenshot comparison requires matching field of view, pose, aspect ratio and exposure where reasonable. Do not claim Blender asset production without an actually available Blender executable and reproducible .blend outputs. No final acceptance before realistic production models and mobile hardware testing.
