# Rev15T2 / Wet grain asphalt and muddy edges

13 November 2026. This is the historical wet-grain revision, superseded for pothole appearance by Rev15T3 after the user's feedback. It added 1.2 m aggregate detail, damp silt and sixteen long side pools while preserving the original sixteen shallow depression meshes. The existing lighting and exposure were retained; screen ray reflections were enabled for the wet surface.

The complete 51-scene, 102-packed-image master was uploaded as thirteen independently verified parts. Native-Master-Parts.json records the Drive IDs, sizes and checksums; the full file SHA256 is 33a2e589654ed8aaed1c5abee13111630118c24bff054331f2891160abee8fdb. Its source scripts, textures, PDF, captures and evidence are carried in the final Rev15T3 source checkpoint. Separate T2 native additions and runtime GLB remain historical artifacts. Use the current ground handoff and Rev15T3 review for the corrected potholes.
