## Materials and source

The black brick texture and edited transparent team compositions were made with `image_gen.imagegen`, using the existing illustrated composites to retain the people and portrait style. New lettering and layout are baked into each team's cohesive PNG; the artwork planes and wall material remain editable in Blender. Per-person labels are not separate native text objects.

The wall uses a worn black-painted masonry texture. The same world-space luminance-derived bump is applied beneath mural paint, keeping mortar and grain aligned through the portraits and titles. The relief is derived from the texture; it is not a scanned height map. No extra review lights were added. The private Three.js viewer reconstructs the same wall texture/relief mapping.

The complete packed master is `delivery/Gullyverse-Rev15K-Black-Brick-Murals.blend`. It contains 41 scenes, including all 40 previous scenes with exactly the same object memberships. Original non-art objects, world and light energies are preserved. The original Rev15J1 file is unchanged and remains available in its earlier verified checkpoint.
