Actual-browser images. The continuous C5.1 geometry, collision, lighting, floor and venue remain unchanged. C8 is a private material preview on existing Wall_15, not recovered C6.2 geometry or new physical relief.

Historical comparison limitation

The original approved C8 report is available. Its companion 162-page screenshot/Evidence ZIP was not accessible in this task. These images compare preserved C5.1 with the reconstruction. They are not labelled as historical C8 screenshots, and pixel matching remains unverified. The original mask formula and C6.2 host are also missing.
