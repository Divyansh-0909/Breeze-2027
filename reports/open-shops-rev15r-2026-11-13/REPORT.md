# Five open shops, informed by Indian market photographs

The five existing open gully shops had repeated sparse carton shelves and blank back panels. Rev15R adds distinct arrangements for kirana provisions, chai and biscuits, household essentials, mobile repair, and snacks with cold drinks. Sources include photographs of Dharavi kirana stores and service signage, a Varanasi tea stall, and a Colaba roadside tea stall. Linked sources and image provenance are retained in `references/sources.json` and the PDF. The repair bench is a modeled interpretation; its photograph provides storefront signage reference.

New details include packed back racks, attached hanging sachet strips, rice sacks, jars, a scale, drink crates, a tea kettle and urn, glasses and saucers, phones, a repair mat, parts trays, a soldering station, a bench lamp and cable loops. Existing generic beige cartons are hidden only in the new scene. Separate object material slots repaint the five back panels. Original source meshes, materials and files remain intact. Counter goods were checked and lowered onto the existing worktops after the first render review.

The original night exposure, world and haze are carried forward, together with the authorized Team light matched to Events. Crowd members use their original detailed Aftermovie meshes for native review. Five local 55 W shelf practicals illuminate shop stock; existing optional Rev05B supports remain off. New geometry stays within the open shop footprints and outside the central lane. All 189 crowd members, eight mural slides, 13 closed fronts and 46 historical scenes are retained.

The PDF shows six credited research photographs, all five matched before/after camera views under inspection lights, and three actual night views. Original unbranded package textures are generated from source; no reference photo or business branding is pasted into the scene. The full packed native master, a smaller additions library, reproducible sources, reference images, captures and evidence are backed up in the new destination account when receipts show PASS. The complete native master is stored in numbered verified parts; RESTORE.md explains reassembly.

This is a native environment revision, not a deployed website change. Quarry movement/rendering files and production routes remain unchanged. The added geometry still needs a dedicated browser export and performance review before live integration. No commit, push, merge or deployment occurred.
