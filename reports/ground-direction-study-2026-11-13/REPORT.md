# Gully road and cypher ground / decision study

13 November 2026. The user wants a pothole-marked road for the gully and suggestions about whether it should continue into the cypher. This phase researches and compares directions; it does not implement a ground change.

Recommendation: continue dusty charcoal asphalt through the existing approach into a wider neighbourhood chowk. Keep a flat, subtly repaired central area with the existing cypher circle in faded cream paint. Concentrate larger potholes, loose aggregate, worn edges and small damp pockets around the perimeter. Join the shops with individually worn concrete thresholds and apron repairs. Preserve the original movement route and existing ramp.

Alternative B is a worn concrete community courtyard, with a visible repair seam at arrival. Alternative C is aged interlocking paving blocks, as seen in the real Dharavi market reference. Both distinguish the cypher more strongly from the asphalt lane; A gives the strongest continuous street feel.
