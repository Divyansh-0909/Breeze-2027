# Rev15T / Continuous asphalt gully and cypher

13 November 2026, Asia/Kolkata. The user selected A / Continuous asphalt and authorized implementation of the ground study. The latest Rev15S shop/sign scene now has a separate Rev15T scene with one continuous asphalt treatment across the flat lane, existing 1:20 ramp, apron and courtyard.

The ground uses original dusty-charcoal artwork, twelve repairs of different ages, five concrete shop-mouth apron patches, sixteen irregular shallow depressions, small rim cracks, 240 low aggregate pieces, three nearly flush drain covers and five shallow damp pockets. Two planned depressions were omitted where they overlapped crowd origins. A third was repositioned to keep its centre clear of an existing carton.

The cypher keeps its existing centre (-5.1, 25.0) and 4.3 m radius. Its subtly resurfaced centre remains flat. The worn cream ring is painted flush into the world atlas; the old 48 raised arc pieces are hidden only in the new scene. Historical scenes retain their previous objects and materials.

All four floor footprints and original boundary vertices are preserved. Their new surfaces retain the original grade, with the local shallow defects outside a protected central 2.8 m walking strip and outside the protected cypher centre. Fresh-open ray checks passed at 471 route points, 320 cypher points, the nine samples across three joins, all sixteen defect centres and all 189 crowd origins. Sampled defect depths are approximately 3.4–6.5 cm. This validates the native surfaces, not a new production movement controller.

The packed master contains 49 scenes, including the 48 historical scenes, and 95 packed images. All 189 original LOD0 crowd meshes, eight mural states, five open-shop interiors, bilingual signs and native night lighting remain. The Rev15S source file SHA256 is unchanged. Original quarry movement/input/navigation/rendering files and production source paths remain unchanged.

The separate GLB ground asset has 13 nodes, 8 materials, 3 embedded images and 71,068 triangles. Four top-surface materials bind the world colour/roughness atlas to UV0 and the repeating 2 m aggregate normal tile to UV1. The standalone ground viewer passed three views, eleven route samples, a one-canvas check and zero page/console/WebGL errors. It records 22 draw calls on this isolated asset. No full-world performance or physical-hardware claim is made.

The six-page PDF contains ten actual native captures: matched lane, approach and cypher before/after pairs, a pothole close-up, an overview and two unchanged native night views. The comparison views use the same temporary inspection lighting, labelled separately from the actual night views. No scene lighting was saved from the inspection setup.
