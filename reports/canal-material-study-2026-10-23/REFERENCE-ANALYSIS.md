# Dried-canal material direction

The two source images were extracted from the actual current shared Google Doc. They are phone screenshots; the black UI, capture glare and flash-like near-wall exposure are not a lighting target.

Both show an abandoned, dry, graffiti-marked passage: desaturated grey/greige cement, chalky beige/taupe mineral patches, powdery roughness, shallow pits, thin cracks, stained and chipped plaster. Recesses become dark; exposed areas are paler without becoming polished white. The second image shows a recessed side throat and worn coating around its arch. The first has long sidewalls and an irregular dry floor under a continuous vault.

These observations guide materials only. The existing rocky silhouette, overhangs, opening, 42m route and floor remain locked. This study cannot make an organic rock mesh resemble a regular constructed canal arch without changing the geometry; that mismatch is expected and will be shown, not concealed. Existing faceted joins/visibility limitations remain inherited.

No reference photograph is used as a runtime texture. No new graffiti or ceiling fixtures are added. The seven exact final artist artworks remain pending; this study preserves the existing artwork state.

The eight requested scans are evaluated even where their source reveals an unsuitable pattern: Concrete Wall 006/008 contain construction seams/holes; Worn Plaster contains a recurring joint; Plaster Stone Wall 02 and Rock Embedded Concrete Wall expose arranged stone. Those are reasons to rank them lower, not to omit their browser screenshots. Chipped Concrete is an additional official CC0 scan selected for dry pitting and matte grey tone. The earlier verified Quarry Wall material is retained as a deliberately coarse geological control, not a cement finalist.
