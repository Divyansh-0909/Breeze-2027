# Cave reference: controlled art study

The user-supplied image is retained as an art reference only. It is not a runtime
texture and its illumination is not copied into the production scene.

The defining forms are large rounded, scalloped wall masses, an asymmetrical
vault, thick low shelves and deep local pockets. The left foreground is a broad
exposed stone face; the right wall and overhead stone project into the passage.
Openings between these masses have irregular, eroded lips rather than straight
cut seams. Detail is concentrated at joints; broad, relatively quiet surfaces
remain between them. There are no regular masonry courses or repeated arches.

Colour is muted brown, taupe and neutral grey with sparse stains. Foreground
brightness partly follows camera illumination: it is not evidence for a much
lighter overall albedo. The study keeps the existing dusk scene and its local
analytical cave-spill response. Neutral white inspection light is separately
labelled and never enabled by the normal preview route.

The existing cave is wider than the pictured passage. Its protected2.38m
half-envelope and42m route remain authoritative. Similar protruding forms must
be accommodated outside that envelope; narrowing the route to mimic the photo
is not authorized. Materials alone cannot reproduce its physical silhouettes.

Material-only comparisons use the original Stage3A cave GLB, with every historic
vertex, UV, tangent and LOD unchanged. All proposed square scans use the same
5.395311m comparison tile width at1x, derived from the current C8 primary rock
period;2x doubles that physical period only in the cave. ambientCG's rectangular
4096x2048 scan keeps its2:1 aspect so its texel density is not stretched. Its
native metre scale is unavailable in the provider record and is not invented.

The independent physical pilot occupies only the existing9m Passage2. A newly
researched CC0 Namaqualand cliff is rigidly rotated and uniformly scaled0.85x.
It is fused with a thick closed authoring body through Blender voxel remeshing,
then the original runtime cages are conformed to that actual3D volume with exact
protected borders. This is not a cave scan, a Boolean union, a normalized scan
chart, or the rejected reference-rock/deep-pilot mesh. Nearest-surface runtime
retopology can soften or lose hidden overhangs; clay and parallax evidence must
be judged separately from the material shootout.
