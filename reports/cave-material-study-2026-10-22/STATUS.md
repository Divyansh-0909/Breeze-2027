# Unfinished texture and cave study — uploaded for preservation

The user explicitly requested including this unfinished work in the WIP upload on 22 October 2026. Inclusion does not complete or visually approve this study.

This snapshot retains 69 actual-browser material screenshots, ten candidate scanned PBR families plus the existing-material control, source/provenance metadata, a Blender master, the 9 m optional geometry pilot, authoring scripts, tests and diagnostic results. Historical capture metadata still says RUNNING because the original capture was interrupted; the process is stopped. Failed trials remain evidence. The complete comparison gallery/PDF, complete material shootout, final art assessment and original full regression/performance program remain unfinished.

On WIP, select `?entranceReview=material-study&caveMaterial=rock_01&caveFeatureScale=2` for the experimental material preview. Other IDs are listed in `public/models/gullyverse/cave-material-study/materials.json`. Add `&caveGeometry=pilot` for the optional 9 m geometry experiment. Without these selectors, WIP uses the approved C8 3× material with the selected C9 quarry and existing natural cave, not these experimental materials.

The upload verification exercised an experimental material and the optional pilot, including safe selection and loading, but does not constitute final art approval or physical-device performance acceptance. Continue the original study only after a separate user instruction.
