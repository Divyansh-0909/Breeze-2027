# Clear ground-level house fronts / Rev15O

The user requested removal of the railings from ground-level houses. This revision removes the low guardrails along the walkable courtyard at four remaining house fronts: L2/Events, R1, R3 and B2. These contain 204 connected metal components, bounded by Z7.376–8.344 m beside the Z7.2 m courtyard floor. The upper balcony railings remain.

The railings were combined with upper metalwork in four meshes. Connected-component surveys isolate only the low rails and vertical pickets. Independent mesh copies remove those vertices and faces. Canonical hashes of every retained face, material index and smooth-shading flag match the original retained geometry. No wholesale material deletion is used, so window hardware, poster frames and unrelated metalwork remain. Structural columns, beams, balcony slabs, doors, windows and shutters are unchanged.

`delivery/Gullyverse-Rev15O-Clear-Ground-House-Fronts.blend` is the current full environment, with 45 packed scenes and all 44 prior scene object memberships. The original Rev15N master remains byte-identical. The 189 crowd characters, warm brick Events facade, black Team wall and all eight mural compositions are retained. The Rev15O mural controller is independent of the previous controller. The sponsor LED keeps its 72-cell gaps and 10,676-frame loop.

The source includes the original rail/component survey, exact removal plan, build and fresh-open checks, native render script, screenshot/PDF builder and restore helper. The source and render validation covers the actual saved master rather than only in-memory geometry.
