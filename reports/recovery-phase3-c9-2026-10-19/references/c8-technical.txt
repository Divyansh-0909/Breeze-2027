2. Why C6.2, and why C7 was not reused
The user prefers C6.2's dark, irregular fractured character. Its 35.557 m study area and comparison assets remain read-only. Its known ~4.8×/7× horizontal
source fitting is not approved and is not reused to place new C8 rock bodies.
C7 is retained solely as historical evidence. Its neutral geometry-only views expose manufactured planes, folds and stepped cuts. None of C7's generated cliff
geometry, source-normalized fitting or mathematical loft is used for the new C8 formations. Existing material/cache/navigation infrastructure is reused
because that infrastructure is separate from the rejected modelling method.

6. Pass B/C — native-proportion physical relief, failed integration
Two original source bodies were rigidly oriented into an approximately 10.47 m Z-span inside the lower C6.2 study wall (roughly Z −72.423…−61.951 m,
exposed source Y approximately0.964…8.531 m). Source01 uses uniform1.0× and source02 uniform0.85×, with −5°/+8° local twists. Transform singular
values confirm equal XYZ scale. PCA is used only to choose rigid orientation; there is no source-chart normalization, no depth flattening, no width-only stretch
and no C7 loft/noise construction.
The original cliff surface was closed into a body behind the visitor-facing wall; native source faces were closed behind their captured boundary and Boolean-
unioned into that body. This was intended to remove duplicate visible surfaces rather than leave decorative boulders in front. Original sources/high geometry
and runtime LODs are retained in the editable master. The native source forms occupy only that local band, but closing and decimating the host also changes
mesh data outside it within the retained35.557 m lower study partition. The failed result therefore does not satisfy the intended strictly local visual-
replacement contract. It is retained only as labelled failure evidence; it must not be treated as a completed8–12 m production benchmark. C6.2 itself is
untouched.
This solid-joining method failed. Extruding folded capture surfaces and unioning them with the old undercut host produces redundant internal faces, needle
intersections and non-manifold topology. Local microscopic cleanup repaired some UV/tangent needles, but did not solve the fundamental solid join. We
stopped rather than replacing this with another folded plane, additional noise or another large geometry iteration.
The normal production export path stops on that failure. An explicit GULLYVERSE_EXPORT_FAILED_ART_TEST=1 flag permits diagnostic exports only. The runtime
manifest says productionEligible:false and records the failed topology/tangent contract. The private UI labels it a failed-join diagnostic. glTF syntax
validation does not make this geometry production-valid.
